⚡ About Student Hub

Connecting Students Through Smart Tools

What We Do

Student Hub is an ecosystem of smart tools designed for students. We provide a 100% client-side document scanner that works entirely on your device — no uploads, no servers, complete privacy.

Our mission is to make student life easier by providing free, accessible, and private tools that help you study, organize, and share knowledge.

Privacy First

  • ✅ No Uploads: Everything stays on your device
  • ✅ No Servers: No backend infrastructure
  • ✅ No Tracking: No cookies or analytics
  • ✅ No Account: No login required
  • ✅ 100% Local: All processing happens on your phone

Features

  • 📸 Live Scan: Camera-based document scanning
  • ✂️ Auto-Crop: Automatic edge detection
  • 📚 Multi-Page: Scan unlimited pages
  • 🎨 Filters: B&W, Enhance, Vintage
  • 📄 PDF Export: Create and download PDFs
  • 🖼️ Gallery Upload: Use existing images
  • 📂 Notes Zone: Upload and download study materials

Our Projects

  • 📄 Student Hub Scanner: Document scanner for students
  • 📚 Twarvis School Notes Zone: Upload and download study materials (Render server may take 10-15 seconds to wake up)
  • 🔐 CyberCraft: Encode · Decode · Encrypt · Decrypt
