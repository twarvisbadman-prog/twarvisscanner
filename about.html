What is T-Scanner?

T-Scanner is a free, privacy-focused document scanning tool. Unlike other apps, all processing happens locally on your device. No images are ever uploaded to a server.

Key Features

  • 📸 Live Scan: Real-time camera capture with auto-crop.
  • 🎨 Smart Filters: B&W, Enhance, and Vintage modes.
  • 📚 Multi-Page: Scan unlimited pages into one PDF.
  • 🖼️ Gallery Upload: Convert existing photos to PDF.
  • 🔒 100% Private: Your files never leave your phone.

Our Ecosystem
