ℹ️ About SmartScanner

What is SmartScanner?

SmartScanner is a 100% client-side document scanning application. It uses your device's camera to capture documents, automatically crops them, enhances image quality, and optionally extracts text using OCR — all without ever sending your data to any server.

🔒 Privacy First

✅

No Uploads: Everything stays on your device

✅

No Servers: No backend infrastructure

✅

No Tracking: No cookies or analytics

✅

No Account: No login required

🛠️ How It Works

  1. Camera Access: Uses the getUserMedia() API to access your camera.
  2. Auto-Crop: Uses jscanify to automatically detect and crop document edges.
  3. Image Enhancement: Applies contrast and brightness adjustments for clarity.
  4. OCR (Optional): Uses Tesseract.js to extract text from the scanned image.
  5. Download: Saves the document to your device as PNG, JPEG, PDF, or text file.

📋 Requirements

  • 🌐 Modern browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
  • 📷 Camera (built-in or external)
  • 🌍 Internet connection for initial load (libraries from CDN)
  • 💾 Works offline after first visit (can be installed as PWA)

🧑‍🎓 For Students

Tips for best results:

  • 📐 Place your document on a flat, well-lit surface
  • 📏 Ensure the entire document is visible in frame
  • 📱 Hold your phone steady while scanning
  • 📄 Use a dark background for better edge detection
  • ☀️ Avoid glare and shadows on the document
